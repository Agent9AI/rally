# Connector brand assets

The connector marks in `site/brandmarks/` identify third-party systems that
Rally can integrate with or is evaluating. They do not imply sponsorship,
certification, or endorsement by those companies.

The bundled SVG snapshots were retrieved on 2026-08-30 from the following
public sources:

- Google, Slack, Cloudflare, Stripe, Atlassian, Salesforce: Iconify `logos`
  collection
- GitHub: Iconify `mdi` collection
- BigQuery: Iconify `gcp` collection
- n8n and OpenClaw: Iconify `thesvg-color` collection
- Hyperagent: the product's published `icon.svg`

Product names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. Rally
uses them only for nominative product identification. Before commercial launch,
review each provider's current brand guidelines and replace any asset whose
permitted presentation has changed.

Hermes Agent is represented by the A2A protocol mark in the interface, not by a
claimed Hermes product logo. Prime Intellect is intentionally shown as text-only
execution infrastructure rather than as an available agent connection.
