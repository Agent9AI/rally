Policy

Accountability outside the prompt.

Models propose work. Rally’s deterministic control plane decides what may run, what needs approval, and when a claim is complete.

01

No self-approval

An item reaches done only when a different model family verifies the owner’s work.

02

Least privilege

Every run freezes its allowed systems and tools before a worker receives the job.

03

Bounded recovery

Second Wind may hand a failure to another family once; it never waives independent review.

04

Receipts, not confidence

Evidence, verifier identity, policy decisions, and residual risk stay attached to the run.
